# grep+LLM retrieval will remain under 2s at 500 notes

Based on ripgrep benchmarks and LLM inference speed, the grep_llm retrieval mode
should maintain sub-2-second latency even when the vault grows to 500+ notes.

**Evidence**: ripgrep can search 1000 files in ~50ms. LLM relevance judgment on
20 candidates takes ~1-1.5s. Total ~1.5s, within budget.

**Validation**: On 2026-05-20, check if:
- Vault has 500+ notes
- Average retrieval latency is under 2 seconds
- Relevance ranking accuracy is acceptable (>0.8 F1)
